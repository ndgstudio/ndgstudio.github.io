NDG Studio Publisher

A private publishing tool for one independent video creator.

NDG Studio Publisher is a small application that runs on the creator's own computer. It uploads short videos that the creator made himself to the creator's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook, using each platform's official API.

How it works

  • The creator connects each of his own accounts once, through the platform's official sign-in and consent screen.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the creator before anything is sent.
  • On TikTok, videos are sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator finishes and posts each one in the TikTok app himself.

YouTube

Uploads to YouTube use YouTube API Services (the youtube.upload permission only). By using the app's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

Only the creator who operates it. It has no public sign-up, no other users, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
