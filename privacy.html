Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy explains what NDG Studio Publisher ("the app") does with data. The app is operated by a single independent creator for his own accounts only. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Account authorization. When the creator connects his own TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Facebook account, the platform gives the app an access token and basic account information (such as the account ID and username) so it can confirm which account it is posting to.
  • Content the creator uploads. The video files, captions and hashtags the creator chooses to publish.

How it is used and stored

  • Tokens and account IDs are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a file readable only by his user account. They are never published, sold or shared.
  • Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the creator selects, through that platform's official API, and only after the creator approves each post.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other person. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the creator's own YouTube channel. It requests only the youtube.upload permission. It does not read, store or analyze any other YouTube data, such as other channels, viewers, comments or statistics. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone other than the platform a video is being published to.

Cookies and device storage

The app and this website do not use cookies. The app stores its access tokens only in a local file on the creator's own computer, as described above.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings: for YouTube and Google, at the Google security settings page; for TikTok, in Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions; for Instagram and Facebook, in Settings, Apps and websites. Revoking access, or deleting the local token file, removes the app's access immediately, and the stored token is deleted. To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.
